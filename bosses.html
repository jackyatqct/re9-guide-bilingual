MAIN STORY

Story Bosses

Boss #1 · Wrenwood Hotel
CHUNK
Heavily mutated former security guard. First major boss.
Leon Grace (alternate) 2 Phases
Moderate Use: Shotgun
Weak Points
🎯
Phase 1: Exposed chest cavity — shoot during his charge wind-up animation.
🎯
Phase 2: Glowing mutation on back — use Flash Grenades or shoot legs to force a 180.
Drops & Rewards
Hatchet: Mortal Edge (Leon)
Chunk's Keycard — exit the Hotel basement
Shotgun Shells ×3 (scattered around arena)
Strategy

Phase 1Stay mobile. Shoot the chest during the pull-back animation. Two shotgun blasts end Phase 1.

Phase 2Throw Flash Grenade to stagger, sprint around to his back, dump shotgun rounds into the glowing mass.

Grace players: Craft 3 Hemolytic Injectors and inject them when the back mutation is exposed — instantly ends Phase 2 without using any gun ammo.
Boss #2 · Raccoon City — RPD Chase + Arena
MR. X
Tyrant-series B.O.W. Relentlessly pursues Leon through the RPD.
Leon only Chase Phase Arena Phase
High Difficulty Save: Explosive Rounds
Weak Points
🎯
Chase Phase: No weak point — do NOT waste ammo. He cannot be stopped.
🎯
Arena Phase: Mouth during punish windows. Back cyst while downed (highest damage window).
💥
Environmental: Explosive canisters around the arena deal ~3× normal gun damage.
Drops
RPD Parking Garage Key
Requiem Rounds ×4
Unlocks East Raccoon City
Strategy

ChaseDo not waste ammo. Run to the upper arena. Use ladders and stairs to create distance while collecting ammo from side rooms.

ArenaUse ladders to force movement angles for mouth shots. Kite near explosive canisters at half health. Dump ammo into back cyst when he's downed — ~3 second window.

Boss #3 · Outside RPD — Final Tyrant Form
SUPER TYRANT
Mutated final form of Mr. X. Open-area fight outside the RPD.
Leon 3 Phases Time-Sensitive
High Difficulty Use: Magnum + Rockets
Weak Points
🎯
Ph. 1–2: Exposed heart on chest. Aim straight on.
🎯
Ph. 3: Shoot arm blade (~12 handgun rounds) to break it — heart re-exposed.
🚁
At 25% HP: Helicopter drops Rocket Launcher ×1 — pick it up immediately (despawns in 30s).
Drops
Raccoon City Survivor Badge
ARK Access Code
Achievement: "Terminator"
Strategy

Ph. 1–2Dump all 5 Magnum rounds into his chest heart. Keep moving — his grab is instant-kill on Insanity.

Ph. 3Shoot the arm blade to shatter it, then switch back to Magnum for the exposed heart.

RocketAt 25% HP pick up the Rocket Launcher immediately — one shot to the chest ends the fight.

Insanity: Prioritize dodging grabs over dealing damage. Getting grabbed is an instant death.
Boss #4 · ARK — Scripted Encounter
MUTATED EMILY
Mandatory scripted encounter. Affects ending branch.
Leon Scripted Ending Branch
Low Difficulty ⚠ Affects Ending
Strategy & Story Note

Scripted encounter — no gameplay punishment. When prompted, shoot mutated Emily to proceed. Do NOT reload a save here.

On the Release Elpis true ending path, Leon clarifies he did not hit vitals and Emily survives. Shooting her does NOT cause the bad ending — it is required for the good one.

Common mistake: Do NOT reload a save here. The ending is determined by the Final Puzzle, not this encounter.
Boss #5 · Rhodes Hill — Grace Campaign
THE KEEPER
Close-quarters duel. Parry-centric fight. 2 phases.
Grace only Parry Mechanic 2 Phases
Hard Required: Hatchet
Weak Points
🎯
Phase 1: After successful parry — 1-second hatchet window. Sharpen hatchet during every pause.
🎯
Phase 2: Left side exposed during 3-hit combo finishers.
Drops
Keeper's Key — unlocks Sub-Basement
Blood Vial ×6
File: The Keeper's Journal
Strategy

Phase 1This is a duel, not an arena fight. Parry window is 0.3s before impact. Immediately answer with hatchet. Sharpen every time he repositions.

Phase 2He adds unparryable grabs telegraphed by a left shoulder dip. On shoulder dip — dodge right, then punish his left side during the 3-hit combo finisher.
