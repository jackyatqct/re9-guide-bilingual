01 Hotel Lobby & Arrival Leon — Combat intro ▼
  • 01
    After the opening cutscene, take control of Leon at the hotel entrance. Head through the main doors — the front desk area is your first exploration zone.
  • 02
    Mr. Raccoon #01 is on the front desk shelf behind the check-in monitor. Shoot it from the entrance — face-height, easy to see.
  • 03
    Open Safe #1 behind the reception counter. Code: R36 · L08 · R52 (Normal). Contains Shotgun Shells ×8 + Handgun Ammo ×12.
  • 04
    Examine the guest register on the desk — File #1. Background lore for the hotel outbreak.
  • 05
    First infected enemy appears in the lobby hallway. Practice the parry timing here — 0.3 seconds before impact. You'll need it for boss fights.
  • 06
    Proceed through the east door toward the Dining Room. Pick up the Typewriter Ribbon from the table near the hall entrance — save resource on Insanity/Classic.
Key Items
File #1 — Guest Register Handgun Ammo ×8 Shotgun Shells ×8 (safe) Typewriter Ribbon
02 Dining Room, Parlor & Roulette Puzzle Leon — First puzzle ▼
  • 01
    Enter the Dining Room. Mr. Raccoon #02 is on the top shelf of the wine rack, far left corner. Look up.
  • 02
    Clear 3 infected. Use hatchet finishers on staggered enemies to conserve ammo — headshot to stagger, then press melee for the hatchet kill.
  • 03
    Small potted plant at the reception left side — examine to get Antique Coin #01.
  • 04
    Enter the Parlor. Examine the painting above the fireplace first to read the color sequence, then interact with the wheel.
  • 05
    Roulette solution: RED — BLACK — RED — RED — BLACK. Reward: Parlor Key + Antique Coin #04.
  • 06
    In the Parlor: Antique Coin #05 (bar counter) and Antique Coin #06 (destroy the porcelain container behind the bar).
  • 07
    Use the Parlor Key to open the back room — take the W-870 Shotgun. Most important early-game weapon.
Tip: Spend Antique Coins at the Parlor lockers now. Buy the Hip Pouch first (3 coins) — extra inventory slots make the entire hotel section easier.
03 Upper Floors (2F – 4F) Leon — Exploration ▼
  • 01
    Take the main staircase to 2F. Two infected in the hallway — shotgun the closer one, hatchet-finish the second.
  • 02
    Room 204 (unlocked): First Aid Spray inside. Room 207 is locked — needs the Basement Master Key. Note the location.
  • 03
    3F Manager's Office has a typewriter save point — save here before proceeding, especially on Insanity/Classic.
  • 04
    4F east end emergency exit leads to the roof.
  • 05
    Mr. Raccoon #05 is on the roof northeast corner near the water tower base. Shoot before heading down.
Don't miss: Room 207 requires the Basement Master Key. Come back after getting it — contains the Shotgun Stock upgrade.
04 Hotel Basement & Generator Room Leon — Pre-boss prep ▼
  • 01
    Take the stairwell to B1. Infected here react to sound — hold crouch-walk to sneak past the first two and avoid unnecessary combat.
  • 02
    Mr. Raccoon #03 is in the Generator Room on the pipe junction near the east wall — it's behind the large pipe. Easy to miss.
  • 03
    Take the Basement Master Key from the hook on the generator room wall. Use it to unlock Room 207 upstairs.
  • 04
    South storage room: destroy the cardboard box near south shelving — Antique Coin #03 inside.
  • 05
    Optional: The Amalgam boss is in the south locked room. Drops Crimson Skull Gem worth 12,000 CP — fight it if you have spare shells.
  • 06
    Typewriter just before the Chunk arena — save here. You want at least 12 Shotgun Shells and 2 Flash Grenades going into the boss fight.
05 Boss Fight: Chunk Leon & Grace — Area boss ▼
  • 01
    Phase 1: Stay mobile. Chunk telegraphs his charge 1.5s before executing — fire 2 shotgun blasts into his exposed chest during the pull-back animation.
  • 02
    After ~4 shotgun blasts, he enters Phase 2 — a glowing mutation appears on his back. Do NOT keep shooting the front.
  • 03
    Phase 2: Throw Flash Grenade to stagger him, sprint around to the back, dump 3–4 shotgun rounds into the glowing mass. Repeat to finish.
  • 04
    Grace players: Craft 3 Hemolytic Injectors from zombies outside the boss room. Inject all 3 when back mutation is exposed — instantly ends Phase 2.
  • 05
    After Chunk dies: loot Chunk's Keycard from his body. Leon also receives Hatchet: Mortal Edge. Use the keycard on the locked door to proceed.
Full boss guide: See the Chunk Boss Strategy page for Insanity difficulty tips.

01 Reception & Blood Crafting Tutorial Grace — Mechanic intro ▼
  • 01
    Grace arrives at the care center entrance. Unlike Leon, her pistol ammo is limited — prioritize stealth over direct combat wherever possible.
  • 02
    Mr. Raccoon #06 is on the left window of the reception area, facing outside. Shoot it immediately from the entrance hallway.
  • 03
    Blood Crafting tutorial triggers here — kill the infected behind reception to collect Infected Blood ×3. Craft a Blood Coagulant to complete the tutorial.
  • 04
    Waiting room right-side chair — crouch and examine floor for Antique Coin #07.
  • 05
    Typewriter save point is in the side room off the reception lobby. Save here — the care center is large.
Grace players: Don't skip blood collection. Infected Blood is your primary crafting resource. Kill enemies in every room before moving on.
02 East Wing (2F) & Nurse's Station Grace — Exploration & safe ▼
  • 01
    Take the Ward A stairwell to 2F. Use the nursing cart as cover to sneak past the first two infected in the hallway.
  • 02
    Open Safe #2 behind the nurses' desk: L22 · R50 · L14 (Normal). Contains Ink Ribbon ×2 + First Aid Spray.
  • 03
    Antique Coin #08 is on the nurses' station counter, right side.
  • 04
    Mr. Raccoon #07 is inside a medical supply cart — requires the East Wing Keycard from the Quartz Door puzzle. Come back for it later.
  • 05
    Exit through the connecting corridor north to reach the Central Hall, where the Quartz Door is located.
03 Director's Office & Quartz Door Puzzle Grace — Key puzzle sequence ▼
  • 01
    Director's Office is on 3F West Wing. A Banshee-type enemy patrols the 3F landing — back away to trigger its patrol, then slip past when it turns.
  • 02
    Open Safe #3 in the Director's Office: R04 · L72 · R38 (Normal). Contains Hip Pouch — expands inventory by 2 slots.
  • 03
    Mr. Raccoon #08 on the Director's bookshelf — 3rd shelf from top, right side. Scan carefully.
  • 04
    Solve the puzzle safe in the adjacent Chairman's Office to collect Moon Quartz and Antique Coin #09.
  • 05
    Return to the Quartz Door in Central Hall. Insert Moon Quartz and input: Moon → Sun → Star → Moon. Reward: East Wing Keycard + Upgrade.
  • 06
    Backtrack to 2F East Wing to collect Mr. Raccoon #07 from the supply cart using the East Wing Keycard.
04 Sterilization Chamber & Basement Grace — Resource-critical section ▼
  • 01
    Take the service elevator from 1F to B1. Sterilization Chamber is straight ahead — a large enemy patrols. Sneak along the left wall to avoid it.
  • 02
    Open Safe #4 on the window side: R10 · L90 · R20 (Normal). Sneak past the enemy to reach it. Contains: Stabilizer.
  • 03
    Mr. Raccoon #09 is on a trolley in the far corner of the Sterilization Chamber, near the enemy.
  • 04
    Chemical Storage room (south corridor): pick up the Bottle of Acid — used to dissolve the Sub-Basement door lock.
  • 05
    B2 Morgue (via service stairs): Antique Coin #10 on the central table, and Mr. Raccoon #11 inside the third-row-from-bottom left drawer.
  • 06
    Use the Acid Bottle to dissolve the Sub-Basement door lock. This leads to the Keeper boss fight area.
05 Boss Fight: The Keeper Grace only — Parry duel ▼
  • 01
    The Keeper is a close-quarters duel. Your hatchet and parry system are your primary tools — guns are inefficient here.
  • 02
    Phase 1: Parry window is 0.3s before impact. Successful parry → immediately strike with hatchet (1-second window). Sharpen during every pause.
  • 03
    Phase 2: He adds unparryable grabs telegraphed by a left shoulder dip. Dodge right on shoulder dip, then punish his left side during the 3-hit combo finisher.
  • 04
    After defeating him, collect Keeper's Key — unlocks the Sub-Basement storage room with Mr. Raccoon #10 and Antique Coin #11.
Full boss guide: See The Keeper Boss Strategy for complete phase analysis.

06 Sub-Basement & Exit Grace — Area wrap-up ▼
  • 01
    Use the Keeper's Key to access the flooded storage room. Mr. Raccoon #10 is on the walkway north wall, just above the waterline.
  • 02
    Flooded storage shelf: Antique Coin #11.
  • 03
    Take the ARK access elevator at the end of the Sub-Basement. Interact with the terminal — cutscene plays, transitioning to Raccoon City.

01 Raccoon City Streets Leon — Open exploration ▼
  • 01
    Leon lands in the ruins of Raccoon City. Explore side streets north and south before heading to the RPD to collect supplies.
  • 02
    Mr. Raccoon #12 is on a fire escape platform 2F level in the NW building cluster. Shoot it from across the street.
  • 03
    Newspaper box near the first barricade — shoot to open — Antique Coin #12 inside.
  • 04
    Merchant's Stall in the east alley. Upgrade shotgun Reload Speed — best value before entering the RPD.
02 Raccoon Police Department Leon — Mr. X begins pursuit ▼
  • 01
    Enter the RPD main lobby. Mr. Raccoon #13 is on top of the RPD crest mosaic — look down from the entrance. Shoot it before proceeding.
  • 02
    First aid kit on the lobby wall — Antique Coin #13 inside.
  • 03
    Head down to Generator Room (B1) and solve the Power Grid Puzzle: flip switches in order C → A → D → B. Wrong order triggers alarm + 2 enemies.
  • 04
    With power restored, open Safe #5 in the Evidence Room: R28 · L66 · R42 (Normal). Contains Magnum Rounds ×5 — SAVE THESE FOR SUPER TYRANT.
  • 05
    Mr. Raccoon #14 is inside Evidence Box #7 — interact with the box itself, not the shelf surface.
  • 06
    Officer corpse in the 1F east hallway holds the RPD Armory Key — opens the Armory for Antique Coin #14.
  • 07
    Entering the 2F Security Room triggers the Mr. X chase sequence. Confirm all items collected before entering.
Save the Magnum Rounds! They're for the Super Tyrant — the Magnum is the most efficient weapon against his exposed heart.
03 Boss: Mr. X Chase & Arena Fight Leon only — Two phases ▼
  • 01
    Chase Phase: DO NOT waste ammo. Mr. X cannot be stopped. Run toward the upper-level arena, collecting ammo from side rooms as you go. Use ladders to create distance.
  • 02
    Mr. Raccoon #16 is on the NF Building Rooftop during the chase — NW corner near the A/C unit. This is timed — shoot it while running or miss it permanently.
  • 03
    Arena Phase: Use ladders to control movement angles. Shoot his mouth during punish windows (after big swing attacks).
  • 04
    When Mr. X goes to one knee, sprint to his back and dump ammo into the back cyst — ~3 second window before he stands.
  • 05
    At half health, kite him near the explosive canisters in the arena corners — they deal ~3× normal gun damage when he's adjacent.
04 Underground Sewers Leon — Pre-final boss prep ▼
  • 01
    After defeating Mr. X, take the Parking Garage Key from his body. Exit through the RPD parking garage to reach the sewer access.
  • 02
    Mr. Raccoon #15 is in the sewers on a ledge above the first water valve puzzle — jump up using the crates to the east.
  • 03
    Open Safe #6 in the Sewers Control Room: L12 · R84 · L56 (Normal). Contains Scope attachment for the Sniper Rifle.
  • 04
    Control panel desk: Antique Coin #15.
  • 05
    The sewer exit brings you to the Central Square — Super Tyrant arena. Save at the typewriter. Stock to 12+ Shotgun Shells and confirm Magnum Rounds are in inventory.
05 Boss Fight: Super Tyrant & Area Exit Leon — Area finale ▼
  • 01
    Before the boss triggers: Mr. Raccoon #17 is at the base of the war memorial statue in Central Square. Grab it now — it disappears after the boss fight starts.
  • 02
    Phases 1–2: Dump all 5 Magnum rounds into his exposed chest heart. Move laterally at all times — his grab attack is instant-kill on Insanity.
  • 03
    Phase 3: He shields his heart with his arm blade. Shoot the blade (~12 handgun rounds) until it shatters — heart re-exposed, finish with Magnum.
  • 04
    At 25% HP: Helicopter airdrop lands a Rocket Launcher ×1. Pick it up immediately — despawns after 30 seconds. One rocket to the chest ends the fight.
  • 05
    After the fight: collect Raccoon City Survivor Badge and ARK Access Code from his remains. Cutscene plays — transition to The ARK.

01 ARK Hangar & Entry Both characters — Area arrival ▼
  • 01
    Both Leon and Grace arrive at the ARK separately. The Hangar is a large open area — clear it thoroughly. Enemies here are faster and more aggressive.
  • 02
    Mr. Raccoon #18 is on top of a cargo container near the east entrance — climb the smaller containers to reach the top surface.
  • 03
    Red equipment cart near the entrance: Antique Coin #16.
  • 04
    Merchant's Stall in the southwest corner — last upgrade opportunity in the game. Prioritize Magnum damage upgrades if available.
02 Research Labs A & B Both characters — Key safe + collectibles ▼
  • 01
    Mr. Raccoon #19 in Lab A inside the specimen containment unit. The Lab Keycard is found in the same room — check the desk near the entrance.
  • 02
    Mr. Raccoon #20 in Lab B on the typewriter desk — right at the save point. Save first, then collect it.
  • 03
    Lab A sample freezer, second compartment: Antique Coin #17.
  • 04
    Open Safe #7 in Lab B: R92 · L24 · R46 (Normal). Contains Override Manual + Steroids — most valuable safe in the game.
  • 05
    Collect Files #68 and #69 (Product Catalogs) from Lab B — these are the clues for the Final Puzzle. Read them carefully.
03 Control Tower & Point of No Return Both characters — Last collectible chance ▼
  • 01
    Mr. Raccoon #21 on Control Tower Level 3, behind the main console panel — crouch to see it behind the desk.
  • 02
    The ARK Core Corridor leads to the Point of No Return door. Mr. Raccoon #22 is on a pipe junction 20m before the door — last chance on Insanity.
  • 03
    Final checklist before the Point of No Return door: Mr. Raccoons #18–22 ✓ | All Antique Coins ✓ | Files #68 & #69 in inventory ✓ | Ammo stocked ✓
  • 04
    Enter the ARK Core Checkpoint door when ready. This transition is permanent and cannot be reversed.
04 Boss: Mutated Emily & Core Chamber Leon — Scripted encounter ▼
  • 01
    Just past the Point of No Return door: collect Mr. Raccoon #23 on the left wall, and Mr. Raccoon #24 on the second console tier facing Grace's terminal.
  • 02
    Mr. Raccoon #25 is hidden inside the broken monitor on the west wall — shoot the screen to reveal it, then shoot the statue.
  • 03
    The Mutated Emily encounter is fully scripted — no gameplay punishment. When prompted, shoot her. Do NOT hesitate or reload a save. This does NOT cause the bad ending.
  • 04
    After the encounter, both Leon and Grace reconvene at the Final Puzzle terminals.
Common mistake: Players reload thinking shooting Emily causes the bad ending. It does NOT. The ending is 100% determined by the Final Puzzle inputs — not this encounter.
05 Final Puzzle & Game Ending Both characters — Ending branch ▼
  • 01
    Two terminals appear: one for Grace, one for Leon. Both must be solved for the true ending. In solo mode, Leon's auto-locks after Grace completes hers — solve Grace's terminal first.
  • 02
    Grace's Terminal (True Ending): BLOOD → MEMORY → REQUIEM → REBIRTH
  • 03
    Leon's Terminal (True Ending): TRUTH → SACRIFICE → ORDER → RELEASE
  • 04
    Both correct inputs trigger the Release Elpis true ending cutscene — approximately 12 minutes long, showing Emily's survival and the aftermath.
  • 05
    After the credits: New Game+ unlocks (weapons carried over), Insanity difficulty unlocks, and post-game CP farming and infinite ammo systems become accessible.
