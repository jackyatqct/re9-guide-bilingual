Roulette Wheel Puzzle
Wrenwood Hotel · Parlor (Ground Floor)
Solution
RED — BLACK — RED — RED — BLACK
Match the color sequence in the painting above the fireplace
Tip: Red = filled dot ●, Black = empty circle ○. Reward: Parlor Key + Antique Coin #4.
  • 01Enter the Parlor. Examine the painting above the fireplace.
  • 02Note 5 circles — filled (red) or empty (black).
  • 03Interact with the wheel and spin: Red, Black, Red, Red, Black.
  • 04Collect the Parlor Key and Antique Coin.
